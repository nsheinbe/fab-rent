# Fab.Rent

**Local manufacturing equipment and operators.**

Fab.Rent connects shops with underused machines (CNC, laser, 3D print, etc.) and skilled operators so nearby makers and small manufacturers can get parts made without owning the gear.

## Why

A lot of capacity sits idle between jobs. Buyers need reliable local access; shops need utilization. Fab.Rent is the marketplace + workflow layer for that match — not a factory of its own.

## Status

Greenfield. Repo shell only — product shape and first slice TBD.

## Principles

- **Local first** — geography and lead time matter more than global lowest bid
- **Machines + operators** — capacity without skilled hands is half a product
- **Supplier relationships are the moat** — trust, quality, and repeat bookings beat SEO spam
- **Open where it helps** — clear APIs/data for shops; no dark lock-in on buyer history they should own

## Non-goals (v0)

- Owning or financing the machines
- National same-day logistics as v1
- Competing as a captive print farm
